Quadcopter PID sim

A 2D drone with the same cascaded control structure most flight controllers use. Click in the sky to move the target, change the gains, and see what each term does.

Click or tap to set a target. The dashed line is the pitch the position loop asks for.

Options
Physics at 1 kHz, control at 200 Hz. Rotor lag 40 ms, thrust-to-weight about 2. Source: github.com/WizzardOfOdds/quadcopter-pid-sim
